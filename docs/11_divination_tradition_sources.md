# Divination Tradition Source Baseline

This file records the historical and terminological baseline used to organize
Vol31-Vol41. The sources establish terminology and historical context only.
They do not establish psychological validity or predictive accuracy.

## Indian astrology

- The Indian tradition distinguishes graha, rashi, bhava, nakshatra, and timing
  techniques. Transliteration and technique selection still vary by region and
  school.
- Historical astronomy context: https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0101063

## Zi Wei Dou Shu

- The catalog uses the twelve-palace, fourteen-major-star, auxiliary-star, and
  four-transformation organization common to modern reference systems.
- Origin claims are stored as tradition rather than verified biography because
  the textual history and lineage claims are contested.
- Historical orientation: https://wudang.org/chinese-metaphysics/zi-wei-dou-shu/origins-history

## Nine Star Ki

- The nine stars combine numerical, color, five-phase, palace, direction, and
  calendar correspondences. Japanese schools differ on calendar boundaries and
  several directional calculations.
- General reference: https://kotobank.jp/word/%E4%B9%9D%E6%98%9F-52100

## Sukuyo

- The catalog separates the 27-mansion system, the 28-mansion variant, seven
  luminaries, nine luminaries, historical calendars, and modern calculation
  methods.
- Academic historical overview:
  https://www.waseda.jp/flas/rilas/assets/uploads/2015/12/753ec54c489a6911ebd07248d4624b06.pdf

## Numerology and name divination

- Numerology systems are not interchangeable; each letter-value table,
  reduction rule, alphabet, and name version must be recorded.
- Japanese name divination likewise requires a declared glyph dictionary,
  stroke-count convention, script, reading, and school.
- Japanese sources disagree on modern versus traditional glyph counts and on
  some outer-grid calculations. The runtime therefore accepts declared stroke
  counts instead of silently selecting a dictionary. The one-character virtual
  stroke is an explicit switch rather than an assumed universal rule.
- Japanese glyph-count variation overview:
  https://allabout.co.jp/gm/gc/472932/
- Japanese court record discussing five-grid terms and the virtual-one method:
  https://www.courts.go.jp/assets/hanrei/hanrei-pdf-11485.pdf
- Comparative number-symbolism context:
  https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/61673/chapter-abstract/549685164

## Feng shui

- The catalog separates form-based landscape analysis from compass-based
  direction and time calculations. It also separates cultural interpretation
  from building safety, environmental health, and professional design advice.
- Academic history: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/an-introduction-to-feng-shui/brief-history-of-feng-shui/463E07D18D67257B821EE9AC062B6F9D
- Historical geography overview:
  https://press.uchicago.edu/books/hoc/HOC_V2_B2/HOC_VOLUME2_Book2_chapter8.pdf
